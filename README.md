<summary><b>📂 CASE #003 — QuestGo</b> &nbsp;🚧 <i>In development</i></summary>
<br>

**Classification:** Hyperlocal student-to-student errand marketplace for CIT-U

**How it works:** *Requesters* post **Quests**, *Runners* complete them for cash at campus meet-up points.

**Built-in safeguards:**
- 🔐 Verified student accounts through CIT-U email
- 🔒 Quest details locked after acceptance
- ✅ Mutual, staged completion confirmation
- 🚫 Cancellation tracking with restrictions after repeated cancels
- 📍 Approved campus meet-up locations only

**Interesting problem solved:** making sure only **one** Runner can accept a Quest, handled at the database level.
